<h1 align="center">Hi! I'm Henrique Targino :)</h1>
<h3 align="center">Data Scientist | AI Engineer | Computer Vision</h3>

At 10 years old, during a school activity, the teacher asked what we wanted to be when we grew up. My answer on a piece of paper was simple and direct: **"I want to be IT"**. 

My curiosity to understand how things worked "behind the screens" guided me from an early age. Time passed, and that desire became my reality. Just before turning 18, right after finishing high school, I dove headfirst into the Data Science Bootcamp at [TripleTen Brazil](https://henriquetargino.github.io/Portfolio/images/certificado_tripleten.pdf).

Today, at 19 years old, I work professionally as a **Data Scientist** and **AI Engineer**. In my day-to-day, I work on developing and deploying Machine Learning models focused on prediction and recommendation systems. 

Although I work across the entire data pipeline, my true fascination is **Computer Vision**. The fact that we can teach a machine to look at the world and distinguish a human hand from a cup is something that has always amazed me. Throughout my journey, I have learned and applied various stages and technologies in building projects:

- ⛏️ **Data extraction, cleaning, and processing** _(with **Python**, **Pandas**, and **NumPy**)_;
- 📊 **Data visualization and analysis** _(with **Matplotlib**, **Seaborn**, and **Plotly**)_;
- 🤖 **Predictive Machine Learning and recommendation systems** _(with **Scikit-learn**)_;
- 🧠 **Deep Learning and Neural Networks** _(with **TensorFlow** and **PyTorch**)_;
- 👁️ **Computer Vision for tracking and detection** _(with **OpenCV**, **MediaPipe**, and **YOLO**)_;
- ⚙️ **AI Engineering and Agents** in creating advanced automation flows _(with **PydanticAI, LangChain, and N8N**)_;
- 🔄 **Model deployment and productionization** ensuring scalability.

I emphasize building reliable AI systems by combining solid engineering practices, reproducible experiments, and scalable deployment strategies.

Off the screens and outside the code, I am passionate about traveling and have a tradition of collecting _pins_ from every country I visit, always looking for new perspectives!
